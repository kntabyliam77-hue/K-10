ℹ️ About K-10

Independent language model by L Gamest.

What is K-10?

K-10 is a fully independent language model built and owned by L Gamest. It is not affiliated with OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Meta, or any other AI company. K-10 answers only from knowledge its admin trains it on.

How it works

The admin adds topic-and-answer pairs through the Knowledge Panel. When users ask questions, K-10 searches its trained knowledge and finds the best matching response. If nothing matches, K-10 says so honestly.

Fully independent account

K-10 uses its own account system — your name + a PIN you choose. No Google, Microsoft, Meta, email verification, or any third-party service involved. Everything is stored locally in the browser.
